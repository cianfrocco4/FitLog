# Privacy Policy for The Workout Log

**Last updated:** March 2025

The Workout Log ("the app") is a local-first workout tracker. This policy describes what data the app uses and where it stays.

---

## 1. Data We Collect and Use

### Sign in with Apple

To sign you in, we use **Sign in with Apple**. Apple may provide us with:

- An **anonymous identifier** (so we can recognize your device/session)
- Optionally, on first sign-in only: your **email** and **name** (if you choose to share them)

We use this only to manage your sign-in state. We do **not** receive or store your Apple ID password.

### Workout and Exercise Data

All of your **workouts, exercises, sets, and history** are stored **only on your device** (in your iPhone’s local storage). We do **not** upload this data to our servers or sync it to the cloud in the current version of the app.

### Optional AI Features

The app can use an optional AI feature to suggest **form tips** for exercises and **workout suggestions**. If this feature is enabled (e.g. by your organization or build):

- The app may send **only** exercise names, descriptions, muscle groups, and workout structure to a third-party AI service to generate those tips and suggestions.
- **No** identity information (no Apple identifier, email, or name) is sent with these requests.
- Responses are cached on your device so we don’t repeat requests unnecessarily.

If the AI feature is not configured in your build, no exercise or workout data is sent off your device for AI.

---

## 2. Where Your Data Is Stored

- **On your device:** Sign-in state (Apple identifier, and if provided, email and name), all workouts, exercises, and session history are stored locally on your iPhone.
- **Third-party AI:** If you use the optional AI feature, exercise/workout text is processed by a third-party AI provider (e.g. OpenAI) according to that provider’s privacy policy. We do not control their servers or retention.

We do **not** operate our own servers that store your workout or exercise content.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- To **keep you signed in** and recognize your session (Sign in with Apple).
- To **run the app** (workouts, history, rest timers) using data stored on your device.
- To **provide optional AI tips and suggestions** when that feature is enabled, by sending only the exercise/workout text described above.

We do **not** sell your data. The app does **not** show third-party ads.

---

## 4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we use them only to **remind you when a rest period ends** during a workout. Notification preferences are stored on your device and managed by iOS.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

- You can **stop using the app** and **sign out** at any time; sign-out clears the stored Apple identifier, email, and name from the app’s local storage.
- You can **delete the app** to remove all local data (workouts, history, sign-in state) from your device.
- For the optional AI feature: if it is enabled in your build, using the form-tips or workout-suggestion features means exercise/workout text will be sent as described above. You can avoid that by not using those in-app AI features.

If you are in the European Economic Area or other regions with specific privacy laws, you may have additional rights (e.g. access, correction, deletion). Contact us (below) to exercise them.

---

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
