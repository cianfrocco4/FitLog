Privacy Policy for The Workout Log

Last updated: March 2025

The Workout Log ("the app") is a local-first workout tracker. This policy describes what data the app uses and where it stays.

1. Data We Collect and Use

Sign in with Apple

To sign you in, we use Sign in with Apple. Apple may provide us with an anonymous identifier (so we can recognize your device/session) and, on first sign-in only if you choose, your email and name. We use this only to manage your sign-in state. We do not receive or store your Apple ID password.

Workout and Exercise Data

All of your workouts, exercises, sets, and history are stored only on your device. We do not upload this data to our servers or sync it to the cloud in the current version of the app.

Optional AI Features

The app can use an optional AI feature to suggest form tips for exercises and workout suggestions. If this feature is enabled, the app may send only exercise names, descriptions, muscle groups, and workout structure to a third-party AI service. No identity information is sent with these requests. If the AI feature is not configured, no exercise or workout data is sent off your device for AI.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Sign-in state and all workouts, exercises, and session history are stored locally on your iPhone. If you use the optional AI feature, exercise/workout text is processed by a third-party AI provider according to that provider's privacy policy. We do not operate our own servers that store your workout or exercise content.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to keep you signed in, to run the app (workouts, history, rest timers), and to provide optional AI tips and suggestions when that feature is enabled. We do not sell your data. The app does not show third-party ads.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we use them only to remind you when a rest period ends during a workout. Notification preferences are stored on your device and managed by iOS.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can sign out at any time; sign-out clears the stored Apple identifier, email, and name from the app's local storage. You can delete the app to remove all local data from your device. You can avoid the optional AI feature by not using the form-tips or workout-suggestion features. If you are in the EEA or other regions with specific privacy laws, you may have additional rights; contact us to exercise them.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
