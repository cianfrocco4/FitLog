Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone only. Workouts and history are not synced to our servers in this version.

I signed out—did I lose my workouts?
No. Signing out clears sign-in info; your logged workouts stay on the device until you delete the app.

Rest timer didn’t notify me.
Allow notifications for The Workout Log in iOS Settings → Notifications. Also check Focus / Do Not Disturb.
