Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. Workouts and history stay on your device unless you export them or use optional Apple Health features.

Do I have to sign in?
No. You can continue without Sign in with Apple and use the app locally.

I signed out—did I lose my workouts?
No. Signing out clears sign-in info; your logged workouts stay on the device until you delete the app or erase data from the More tab.

What does Erase all app data remove?
Workouts, history, programs, Coach chats, readiness history, body metrics, and progress photos on this device. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you delete them there.

What’s free vs Premium?
Logging, today’s readiness score, widgets, and 14-day history are free. Premium unlocks AI Coach, program generation, readiness trends, advanced analytics, unlimited history, and export. Manage your subscription in More → Subscription or iOS Settings.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open More → Subscription → Restore / Refresh access, or use Restore purchases on the paywall.

Readiness / Apple Health
Connect Apple Health from the readiness card when you’re ready. Scores are general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

Rest timer didn't notify me.
Allow notifications for Workout Log AI in iOS Settings → Notifications. Also check Focus / Do Not Disturb.
